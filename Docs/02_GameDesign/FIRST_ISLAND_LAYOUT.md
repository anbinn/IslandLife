# First Island Layout

Version: T0.1
Owner: PM

## 1. Design Goal

The first island is not a regular square map.

Approach:
Natural irregular island outline.

Reference:
The spatial planning feel of Stardew Valley.

Direction:
- Small world life feel.
- Natural exploration.
- Player driven gradual transformation.

## 2. World Boundary

Definition:
The world uses a Tile Grid.

Proposed size:
- 160x160 Tile world range.

Note:
This represents the logical boundary, not the island shape.

Actual island:
Natural irregular shape, containing:
- Coastline.
- Bays.
- Forest.
- Plains.
- Slopes.
- Highland area.

## 3. Terrain Region Planning

### Player Living Area

Purpose:
- Starting house.
- Farmland.
- Workbench.
- Storage.

Characteristics:
Flat, expandable.

### Forest Area

Purpose:
- Wood.
- Wild resources.
- Exploration.

Characteristics:
Natural distribution. Regular arrangement is forbidden.

### Slope / Highland Area

Reference:
Slope presentation of Stardew Valley.

Characteristics:
- Grass slopes.
- Stone walls.
- Height differences.
- Mine entrance.

Not a large scale 3D mountain.

### Coast Area

Contains:
- Beach.
- Rocks.
- Coastal resources.

Purpose:
Establishes the island identity.

## 4. Initial Access Design

The player does not own the whole island at the start.

Approach:
Progressive unlocking.

Initial access:
Living area plus its surrounding region.

Later access is opened through:
- Tool upgrades.
- Building.
- Exploration.

Unlocked later:
- Deep forest.
- Highland area.
- Special areas.

## 5. Terrain Art Rules

Forbidden:
- Square island.
- Artificially cut boundaries.
- Large scale regular arrangement.

Adopted:
- Irregular coastline.
- Natural vegetation distribution.
- Slope transitions.
- Small scale regional variation.

## 6. Future Expansion Principles

The first island must reserve:
- New building area.
- New resource area.
- New gameplay entry points.

Do not fill the island in a single pass.
